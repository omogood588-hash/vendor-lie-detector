Last updated: June 2025

Short version: We do not store your contracts. We do not sell your data. We do not track you. We collect the minimum possible to make the service work.

What We Collect

WhatWhyHow long
Contract text (extracted from your PDF) Sent to our AI provider (Groq) to generate the risk analysis Not stored by us. Groq may retain for up to 30 days per their policy.
Usage count (stored in your browser only) To enforce the rate limit of 5 analyses per hour Resets every hour. Stored locally on your device only.
Analysis results (stored in your browser only) Caching so the same contract is not re-analyzed unnecessarily 24 hours. Stored locally on your device only. Never sent to our servers.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not store uploaded PDF files anywhere on our servers
  • We do not collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers
  • We do not use advertising tracking cookies
  • We do not use Google Analytics or any third-party analytics
  • We do not sell, share, or trade your data with any third party
  • We do not build profiles or track you across sessions

How We Use Your Information

The only data that leaves your device is the text extracted from your contract PDF. This text is sent to Groq (our AI provider) via an encrypted HTTPS connection to generate the risk analysis. We do not log this text on our servers.

Third Party Services

Groq: We use Groq's API to power the AI contract analysis. When you submit a contract, the extracted text is sent to Groq's servers. Groq's privacy policy is available at groq.com/privacy-policy.

Vercel: Our website is hosted on Vercel. Vercel may collect standard server logs (IP addresses, request timestamps) as part of normal hosting operations. See vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Google Fonts: We load fonts from Google Fonts. Google may log font requests. See policies.google.com/privacy.

Local Storage

If you accept our cookie consent, we store the following in your browser's local storage:

  • Your usage count (for rate limiting) — resets hourly
  • Cached analysis results — expires after 24 hours
  • Your consent preference

If you decline, we do not store anything except your decline preference. The service still works but rate limiting will not persist across page reloads.

You can clear all local storage at any time through your browser settings.

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Since we do not collect personal data, most data rights requests do not apply. However:

  • Right to erasure: Clear your browser's local storage to delete all data we have stored on your device.
  • Right to know: This policy fully describes what we collect.
  • Opt out: Decline the cookie consent banner to prevent local storage use.

Children's Privacy

This Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, contact us through our website.
